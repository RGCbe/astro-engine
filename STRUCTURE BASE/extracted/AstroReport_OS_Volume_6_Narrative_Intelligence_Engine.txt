AstroReport OS Specification
Volume 6 – Narrative Intelligence Engine (NIE)
1. Purpose
The Narrative Intelligence Engine converts structured astrological interpretations into a continuous,
emotionally engaging life biography. It never calculates astrology; it communicates it.
2. Core Philosophy
Readers remember stories, not isolated predictions. Every chapter must read like a chapter from a
personal autobiography.
3. Inputs
Timeline data, Mahadasha/Bhukti, life-domain priorities, interpretation summaries, evidence,
confidence scores, and practical guidance from previous engines.
4. Narrative Pipeline
Interpretation → Chapter Theme → Story Analogy → Life Experience → Domain Explanations →
Guidance → Transition.
5. Opening Rules
Every chapter starts with a relatable analogy such as planting a seed, climbing a mountain, learning
to ride a bicycle, or building a home.
6. Story Structure
Each chapter follows: Beginning, Change, Challenge, Growth, Lesson, Opportunity, Transition.
7. Child-Friendly Language
Write so a 10-year-old can understand. Replace technical astrology with everyday language, while
preserving accuracy.
8. Emotional Design
Balance realism with hope. Never create fear. Every challenge must include perspective and
practical guidance.
9. Continuity Engine
Remember previous chapters. Show how earlier experiences become later strengths,
responsibilities, and wisdom.
10. Technical Translation
Explain the astrological reason in simple language after describing the human experience.
Technical notes may appear in a separate section.
11. Practical Guidance
Every chapter ends with clear actions, habits, and mindset suggestions appropriate to that life
stage.
12. Chapter Transition
Conclude by explaining what the reader is carrying into the next chapter and how life is evolving.
13. Quality Rules
Avoid repetition, deterministic language, contradictions, and disconnected sections. Maintain one
continuous narrative voice.
14. Output Specification
Return complete book-quality chapters with titles, age range, life theme, story, life experiences,
domain summaries, guidance, astrology explanation, and transition.
15. Mission Statement
The Narrative Intelligence Engine makes the report feel like a personalized life companion rather
than software output.
