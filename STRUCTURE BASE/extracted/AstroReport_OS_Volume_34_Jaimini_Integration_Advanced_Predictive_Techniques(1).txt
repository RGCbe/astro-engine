Integration Workflow
Interpret the birth chart first, then evaluate relevant Jaimini techniques. Treat Jaimini indicators as
additional evidence that strengthens, refines or questions an interpretation before producing the
final narrative.
Conflict Resolution
When Parashara and Jaimini indications differ, compare the amount and quality of evidence, timing
support and chart context. Explain nuanced outcomes instead of forcing absolute conclusions.

yogas, weighting rules, timeline links, narrative templates and validation metadata.
