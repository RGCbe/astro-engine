Knowledge Record Template
Each answer stores Question ID, category, intent, required chart evidence, timeline stage,
supporting rules, child-friendly answer, professional answer, reusable answer blocks, confidence
score, cross references and version history.
Population Strategy
Represent every question as an intent linked to structured evidence rather than storing isolated
answers. The engine builds a personalized response from the user's chart and timeline.
