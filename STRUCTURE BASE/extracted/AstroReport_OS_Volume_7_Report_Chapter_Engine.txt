AstroReport OS Specification
Volume 7 – Report Chapter Engine (RCE)
1. Purpose
The Report Chapter Engine defines the exact blueprint for every chapter in the final life report. It
ensures consistency regardless of the reader's chart.
2. Core Principle
Every chapter represents one meaningful period of life defined by the Timeline Intelligence Engine.
Chapters are modular but connected.
3. Standard Chapter Layout
Title, Age Range, Mahadasha/Bhukti, Life Theme, Story Analogy, Life Experience, Personal
Growth, Life Domains, Opportunities, Lessons, Mistakes to Avoid, Hidden Gifts, Astrological
Reason, Practical Guidance, Transition.
4. Dynamic Length
Expand chapters when many important life domains are active. Keep less significant periods
concise.
5. Life Domain Blocks
Each chapter automatically includes only relevant domains such as Family, Education, Career,
Marriage, Wealth, Health, Property, Children, Spirituality and Reputation.
6. Experience Priority
Describe what the person experiences before explaining astrological causes.
7. Continuity Rules
Each chapter references earlier lessons and prepares the next stage of life.
8. Repetition Control
Avoid repeating identical explanations. Summarize recurring themes and highlight what has
changed.
9. Visual Elements
Reserve placeholders for timelines, milestone cards, key lessons, life maps and chapter
summaries.
10. Summary Box
End every chapter with: Main Lesson, Main Opportunity, Main Focus, Suggested Actions.
11. Chapter Types
Support Birth, Childhood, School, Teenage, Higher Education, Career Building, Marriage,
Parenthood, Leadership, Retirement, Wisdom and Legacy chapters.
12. Output Format
Generate structured Markdown/JSON blocks suitable for PDF, web and mobile rendering.
13. Validation
Verify chapter completeness, logical flow, timeline accuracy, domain coverage and narrative
consistency.
14. Mission Statement
The Report Chapter Engine ensures every chapter feels like part of one professionally written
biography while remaining faithful to the astrological interpretation.
