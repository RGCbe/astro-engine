AstroReport OS Specification
Volume 9 – Question Knowledge Base Architecture
(QKBA)
1. Purpose
Define the architecture for a master question repository that allows the report engine to answer
nearly every client question automatically.
2. Design Philosophy
Questions are not stored as plain text alone. Each question becomes structured knowledge
connected to life domains, astrology, timeline and narrative.
3. Question Object
Each question record contains: ID, title, aliases, domain, subdomain, life stage, Mahadasha/Bhukti
relevance, houses, planets, divisional charts, evidence rules, confidence rules, narrative template,
follow-up links.
4. Master Categories
Identity, Childhood, Family, Education, Career, Business, Wealth, Property, Marriage,
Relationships, Children, Health, Travel, Reputation, Spirituality, Purpose, Retirement, Legacy, Daily
Life.
5. Timeline Mapping
Every question is assigned to one or more life stages and is automatically surfaced only when
relevant in the person's timeline.
6. Evidence Mapping
Every question lists the astrological combinations required before it can be discussed, including
planets, houses, yogas, divisional charts and timing engines.
7. Narrative Mapping
Each question references reusable narrative blocks so repeated topics are explained consistently
throughout the report.
8. Cross References
Questions link to related questions. Example: Wealth links to Career, Business, Property,
Investments and Financial Habits.
9. Validation
Ensure no important category is missing, duplicate questions are merged, unsupported conclusions
are rejected and every report receives complete coverage.
10. Expansion Strategy
Support 1000+ questions by allowing unlimited categories, aliases and rule sets without changing
the report engine.
11. Mission Statement
The Question Knowledge Base is the knowledge brain of AstroReport OS. It ensures
completeness, consistency and scalability while keeping the final report natural and timeline-driven.
