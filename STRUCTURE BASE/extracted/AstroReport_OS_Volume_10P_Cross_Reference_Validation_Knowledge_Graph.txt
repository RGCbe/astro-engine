Knowledge Graph Principles
Every question is linked to life domains, timeline chapters, Mahadasha/Bhukti periods, astrological
evidence, narrative templates and related questions. The graph allows the report engine to discover
connections automatically rather than treating each topic independently.
Validation Checklist
Verify domain coverage, timeline continuity, evidence support, narrative flow, confidence
assignment, duplicate detection, conflict resolution and final report completeness before
publication.
