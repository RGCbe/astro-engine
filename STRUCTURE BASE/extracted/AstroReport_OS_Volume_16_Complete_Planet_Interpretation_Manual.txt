Interpretation Framework
Interpret each planet using the same sequence: natural meaning → house placement → sign
placement → dignity → house lordship → aspects → conjunctions → yogas → divisional chart
confirmation → Dasha activation → timeline impact.
Narrative Rules
Describe how the planetary energy is experienced in everyday life before explaining the astrological
reasoning. Use clear language suitable for general readers.
Developer Specification
For every planet maintain: keywords, strengths, weaknesses, life domains, timeline triggers, related
questions, report templates, confidence modifiers and validation rules.
