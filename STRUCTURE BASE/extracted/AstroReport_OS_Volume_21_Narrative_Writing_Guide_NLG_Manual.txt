Writing Principles
Begin with what the reader experiences in everyday life. Explain the astrological reasoning only
after the experience is understood. Prefer short paragraphs, familiar examples and smooth
transitions.
Narrative Templates
Provide reusable templates for introductions, life-stage transitions, challenge explanations,
opportunity descriptions, summaries and guidance sections.
Developer Specification
Store tone profiles, reading-level rules, analogy libraries, glossary entries, reusable paragraph
templates, localization keys and validation checks.
Quality Checklist
Verify readability, consistency, absence of repetition, age suitability, timeline continuity and
evidence accuracy before publishing.
