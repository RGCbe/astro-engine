Decision Principles
Never rely on a single indicator. Prefer conclusions supported by multiple independent factors.
When evidence is mixed, explain both supportive and limiting influences instead of making absolute
claims.

Return structured reasoning including: life domain, evidence summary, timing, confidence score,
narrative theme, guidance and validation status.
