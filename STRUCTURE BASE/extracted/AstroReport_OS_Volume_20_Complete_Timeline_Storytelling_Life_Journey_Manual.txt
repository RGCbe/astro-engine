Storytelling Rules
Write each chapter as a continuation of the previous one. Explain what changes, why it changes,
how the person experiences it, what they learn, and how it prepares them for the next phase of life.
Reader Experience
The report should feel like someone is telling the reader the story of their own life. Use simple
language first, then explain the astrological reasoning in a separate section if required.
Developer Specification
Each chapter stores timeline range, dominant themes, active evidence, emotional tone, narrative
template, transition text, key lessons, guidance and validation checks.
Quality Checklist
Verify chronological continuity, consistent character development, age-appropriate explanations, no
repeated paragraphs, complete timeline coverage and smooth transitions.
