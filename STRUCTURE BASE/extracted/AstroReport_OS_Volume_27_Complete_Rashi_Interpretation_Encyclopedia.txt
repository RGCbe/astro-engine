Standard Rashi Entry
Each sign includes: overview, element, modality, ruler, strengths, growth areas, communication
style, career themes, wealth patterns, relationship style, spiritual lessons, timeline influence, report
template and validation rules.
Interpretation Framework
Interpret every placement as: planet + sign + house + aspects + dignity + Dasha timing + life stage
before producing the narrative.
Developer Specification
Maintain sign IDs, keywords, elemental traits, compatibility metadata, report blocks, confidence
modifiers and cross-references.
