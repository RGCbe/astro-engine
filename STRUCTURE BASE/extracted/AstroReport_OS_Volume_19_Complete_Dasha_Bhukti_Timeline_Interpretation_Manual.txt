Interpretation Sequence
Determine the active Mahadasha, evaluate the Bhukti, confirm with the birth chart, review relevant
divisional charts, consider supporting transits, align with the person's age and life stage, then
generate the timeline narrative.
Timeline Rules
Every Dasha begins a new life chapter only if it creates a meaningful shift in experience. Connect
each chapter to the previous one so the report reads like a biography instead of isolated
predictions.
Developer Specification
Store timing rules, activation logic, narrative templates, confidence modifiers, chapter boundaries,
validation rules and transition logic for every Dasha period.
