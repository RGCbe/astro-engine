Evaluation Sequence
Identify the yoga, validate required conditions, measure planetary strength, confirm with house
lords and divisional charts, determine Dasha activation, assign confidence and generate a
timeline-aware interpretation.
Narrative Rules
Describe how the yoga may influence life experiences before explaining the astrological
combination. Integrate multiple yogas into one coherent life story rather than listing them
separately.
Developer Specification
Each yoga object should include prerequisites, validation rules, life domains, confidence modifiers,
activation logic, related questions, narrative templates and conflict-resolution rules.
