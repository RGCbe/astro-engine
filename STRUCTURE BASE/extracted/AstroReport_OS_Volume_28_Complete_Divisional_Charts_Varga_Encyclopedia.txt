Purpose
This manual standardizes how divisional charts are interpreted and integrated into AstroReport OS.
It defines when each Varga chart should be consulted, how it confirms birth chart indications, and
how its evidence is incorporated into the final report.

Interpretation Sequence
Start with the birth chart (D1), then consult the relevant divisional chart for confirmation. Do not let a
divisional chart override the birth chart without strong supporting evidence.
Standard Entry Template
Each Varga entry should include purpose, calculation reference, life domains, confirmation rules,
planetary interpretation, Dasha interaction, report placement, confidence modifiers and validation
rules.
Developer Specification
Store chart ID, supported life domains, evidence weight, activation rules, cross-references,
reusable narrative templates and report integration metadata.
