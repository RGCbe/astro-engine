Chapter Assembly Rules
Build chapters chronologically. Preserve continuity between chapters, avoid repeated explanations,
and carry forward unresolved themes until they naturally conclude. Each chapter must include
timeline, life domains, evidence summary, narrative, guidance and transition.
Quality Assurance
Before rendering, verify timeline completeness, evidence consistency, narrative flow, question
coverage, formatting, references and confidence scoring.
