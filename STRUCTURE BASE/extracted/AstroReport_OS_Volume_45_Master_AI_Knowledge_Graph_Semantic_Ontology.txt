Ontology Principles
Define every concept once with immutable identifiers. Represent planets, houses, signs,
Nakshatras, yogas, Dashas, events and report blocks as interconnected entities.
Reasoning Traversal
The AI should traverse the graph to collect evidence, resolve relationships, identify timeline triggers,
retrieve narrative blocks and assemble the final report with traceable reasoning.
Developer Specification
Support graph databases, vector search, semantic APIs, ontology versioning, multilingual labels,
audit trails and backward-compatible schema evolution.
