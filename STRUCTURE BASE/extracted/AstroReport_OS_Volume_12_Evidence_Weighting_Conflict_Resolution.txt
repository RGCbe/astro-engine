Conflict Resolution Rules
Never base a conclusion on a single indicator. Prefer interpretations confirmed by multiple
independent sources. When evidence conflicts, explain the dominant trend while acknowledging
limiting influences. Assign High, Medium or Low confidence based on the amount and quality of
supporting evidence.
Reasoning Output
Return: Life Domain, Supporting Evidence, Opposing Evidence, Timeline, Confidence Score, Final
Interpretation and Validation Status.
