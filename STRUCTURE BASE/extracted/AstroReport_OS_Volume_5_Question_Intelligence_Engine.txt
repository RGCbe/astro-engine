AstroReport OS Specification
Volume 5 – Question Intelligence Engine (QIE)
Purpose
The Question Intelligence Engine ensures the report answers nearly every meaningful client
question without presenting a traditional FAQ. Questions are mapped to life domains, life stages
and timeline chapters.
Core Philosophy
People ask questions, but astrologers think in life patterns. Convert every question into a structured
life domain before generating answers.
Question Ontology
Maintain a master library grouped into Identity, Family, Education, Career, Business, Wealth,
Marriage, Children, Health, Property, Travel, Spirituality, Purpose, Reputation, Legacy and Daily
Life.
Mapping Rules
Every question maps to: one or more life domains, chart evidence, Dasha/Bhukti timing, life stage,
narrative priority and confidence score.
Examples
'Will I become rich?' maps to Wealth, Career, Business, Property, Investments, Savings and
Timing. 'Will I marry?' maps to Relationships, Marriage, Family, Timing and Personal Growth.
Priority Engine
At each timeline chapter, answer only the questions naturally relevant to that life stage. Briefly
update other domains only when needed.
Coverage Validation
Before finishing the report, verify that every important life domain has been addressed at least once
and every recurring domain has been updated across time.
Narrative Integration
Never expose the question list to the reader. Blend the answers naturally into the story so the report
feels like a biography rather than an interview.
Developer Interface
Represent each question with metadata: Question ID, Domain IDs, Priority Rules, Evidence
Requirements, Timeline Rules, Narrative Template and Validation Rules.
Mission Statement
The QIE guarantees completeness. Its purpose is to ensure that the reader rarely needs to ask
follow-up questions because the report has already addressed them at the appropriate point in the
life journey.
