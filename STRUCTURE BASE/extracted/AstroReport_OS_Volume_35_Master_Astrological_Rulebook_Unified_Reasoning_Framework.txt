Master Rule Hierarchy
Always interpret the birth chart first. Use divisional charts, yogas, timing systems and advanced
techniques to confirm, refine or qualify conclusions. Avoid conclusions based on a single indicator.
Unified Reasoning Workflow
Collect evidence → assign weights → resolve conflicts → determine timing → calculate confidence
→ build timeline → generate narrative → validate consistency → render final report.
Knowledge Graph
Every rule should reference related planets, houses, signs, Nakshatras, Vargas, yogas, Dashas,
questions, timeline chapters and report templates through a shared knowledge graph.
Developer Specification
Maintain versioned rule IDs, precedence matrices, confidence formulas, validation checks,
reasoning traces and audit logs for every interpretation.
