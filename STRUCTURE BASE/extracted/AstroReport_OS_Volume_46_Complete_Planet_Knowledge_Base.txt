Knowledge Record Template
Every record should contain: unique ID, title, astrological conditions, life domains, timeline impact,
Dasha interaction, supporting evidence, confidence score, child explanation, expert explanation,
reusable narrative blocks and cross references.
Population Strategy
Create atomic knowledge records rather than long articles. The report engine combines multiple
records into personalized chapters based on the user's chart and timeline.
Developer Specification
Support multilingual content, semantic search, embeddings, version history, source attribution,
validation status and AI-ready retrieval metadata.
