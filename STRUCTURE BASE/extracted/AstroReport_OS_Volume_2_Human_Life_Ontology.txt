AstroReport OS Specification
Volume 2 – Human Life Ontology
Purpose
Define everything that can happen in a human life so the report engine understands life before
explaining astrology.
Core Principle
Life → Experience → Question → Astrology → Explanation. Never start with planets; start with the
person's lived experience.
Master Life Tree
Identity, Physical Life, Emotional Life, Mental Life, Family, Education, Friends, Love, Marriage,
Career, Business, Wealth, Property, Children, Health, Travel, Society, Reputation, Spirituality,
Purpose, Legacy.
Life Domains
Identity
Personality, confidence, thinking, communication, leadership, creativity, discipline, motivation,
values, talents, weaknesses.
Physical Life
Birth, body, energy, appearance, fitness, sleep, food, recovery, ageing, lifestyle.
Emotional Life
Love, fear, anger, joy, stress, attachment, resilience, emotional healing.
Mental Life
Memory, intelligence, focus, curiosity, imagination, planning, decision making.
Family
Mother, father, siblings, grandparents, relatives, home, responsibilities, traditions.
Education
School, college, higher studies, mentors, skills, exams, lifelong learning.
Friends
Friendships, networking, teamwork, trust, support, influence.
Love
Romance, attraction, compatibility, commitment, boundaries, healing.
Marriage
Partner, communication, shared goals, finances, family, long-term partnership.
Career
Employment, promotions, leadership, recognition, retirement.
Business
Startup, partnership, management, marketing, expansion, succession.
Wealth
Income, savings, investments, insurance, passive income, financial freedom.
Property
House, land, construction, rental income, relocation.
Children
Parenting, education, responsibilities, guidance, legacy.
Health
Physical and mental wellbeing, nutrition, exercise, preventive care.
Travel
Domestic, international, migration, pilgrimage, business travel.
Society
Community, public image, influence, service.
Reputation
Integrity, respect, authority, recognition.
Spirituality
Faith, meditation, gratitude, compassion, inner peace.
Purpose
Life mission, calling, dreams, contribution, fulfilment.
Legacy
Retirement, wisdom, mentoring, impact, life review.
Domain Relationships
Life domains influence each other. Example: Confidence → Education → Career → Wealth →
Marriage → Leadership → Legacy.
Question Mapping
Every user question maps to one or more life domains. Example: 'Will I become rich?' maps to
Wealth, Career, Business, Property, Investments, Savings, Timing and Decision Making.
Life Stage Mapping
Different domains become more important at different ages. Childhood emphasizes family and
learning; adulthood emphasizes career, relationships and wealth; later life emphasizes wisdom,
purpose and legacy.
Report Rule
The report engine should prioritize the domains that are active for the current life stage and Dasha,
while briefly updating the remaining domains.
Volume 2 Outcome
The report engine now understands the complete structure of human life. Volume 3 will map these
life domains to the Dasha and Bhukti timeline so every chapter answers the right questions at the
right time.
