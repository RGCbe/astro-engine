Knowledge Record Template
Each record includes ID, house number, astrological condition, interpretation, affected life domains,
timeline influence, supporting evidence, child explanation, expert explanation, narrative blocks,
confidence score and cross references.
Population Strategy
Create modular knowledge records for every house condition. The report engine combines multiple
records according to chart context instead of using static articles.
