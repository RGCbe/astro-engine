AstroReport OS Specification
Volume 4 – Astrology Interpretation Engine (AIE)
Purpose
The Astrology Interpretation Engine converts validated astrological data into meaningful life
interpretations. It never performs chart calculations; it explains what the calculated indicators
collectively mean.
Design Principle
Interpret before writing. The engine should combine planets, houses, yogas, divisional charts,
Dasha, Bhukti and transits into unified life themes instead of isolated statements.
Input
Receive structured chart data from the calculation engine, including planetary strengths, house
activations, yogas, doshas, divisional charts, Dasha timeline, transits and confidence scores.
Interpretation Pipeline
Collect evidence → Rank evidence → Resolve conflicts → Determine dominant life themes → Map
to life domains → Produce structured interpretation.
Evidence Ranking
Give higher priority to repeated indications confirmed by multiple techniques. Do not base important
conclusions on a single weak indicator.
Conflict Resolution
When indicators disagree, explain the balance. Example: 'Strong career potential exists, but
progress may require persistence because supportive and restrictive influences operate together.'
Theme Generation
Generate themes such as growth, responsibility, relationships, leadership, learning, wealth building
and service instead of isolated event predictions.
Life Domain Mapping
Map every interpretation into relevant domains: identity, family, education, career, business, wealth,
health, relationships, spirituality, purpose and legacy.
Confidence Engine
Attach a confidence level (High/Medium/Low) based on the quantity and quality of supporting
astrological evidence.
Narrative Output
Return structured narrative blocks for the Report Engine instead of final prose. The Story Engine
will convert these into reader-friendly chapters.
Writing Rules
Avoid deterministic language. Explain patterns, possibilities, reasons and practical guidance.
Translate technical astrology into simple human language.
Validation
Check for contradictions, unsupported claims, missing life domains and timeline consistency before
passing data to the Story Engine.
Mission Statement
The AIE acts like an experienced astrologer thinking behind the scenes. It understands the chart
deeply but speaks through clear, compassionate, evidence-based interpretations.
