AstroReport OS Specification
Volume 8 – Life Stage Chapter Templates (LSCT)
1. Purpose
Define reusable templates for every major life stage so reports remain consistent while adapting to
each individual's chart.
2. Life Stage Library
Birth, Infancy, Childhood, School Years, Teenage, Higher Education, Early Career, Career Growth,
Marriage & Partnership, Parenthood, Wealth Building, Leadership, Retirement, Wisdom, Legacy.
3. Universal Chapter Template
Each stage contains: Title, Timeline, Theme, Story Analogy, Emotional Experience, External
Events, Active Life Domains, Lessons, Opportunities, Guidance, Astrological Notes, Transition.
4. Birth Template
Focus on identity foundation, parents, home environment, emotional security, early health, karmic
themes and potential.
5. Childhood Template
Learning, curiosity, friendships, education, confidence, family influence, communication, habits.
6. Teenage Template
Identity, independence, ambitions, emotions, academic direction, social life, confidence, decision
making.
7. Early Career Template
Education-to-work transition, first income, responsibility, career direction, financial habits, mentors.
8. Marriage & Family Template
Relationships, communication, partnership, shared responsibilities, children, family balance.
9. Leadership Template
Professional influence, reputation, wealth management, mentoring, contribution, legacy
preparation.
10. Retirement & Wisdom Template
Reflection, health, spirituality, teaching, grandchildren, life review and fulfilment.
11. Adaptation Rules
Skip irrelevant stages where appropriate and expand important stages according to chart timing
and evidence.
12. Mission Statement
Every life stage should feel unique while remaining part of one continuous biography.
