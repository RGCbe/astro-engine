Database Schema
Each Nakshatra should include overview, symbolism, deity, ruling planet, four Padas, traits, life
domains, timing rules, narrative templates and multilingual labels.

Nakshatra ID, aliases, Pada records, element, Gana, Yoni, Nadi, Rajju, keywords, evidence
weights, report blocks, graph edges and version history.
