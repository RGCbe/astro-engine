## 1. Executive Summary

- **Deliverable type:** Serious Jyotish work is either a **live consultation** (60�120 minutes, Zoom/phone/in-person) with optional written follow-up, or a **standalone PDF report** (typically **30�100+ pages**, sometimes 200+ for software-generated �life reports�) emailed within 24�72 hours ([Palash Thakur](https://palashthhakur.com/comprehensive-astrology-report), [AstroSight guide](https://astrosight.ai/kundli/personalized-kundali-report), [Vedic Astro Analyst Life Report](https://lifereport.vedicastroanalyst.com/)).
- **Structure, not slogans:** Reports open with **verified birth data and chart tables**, then **natal promise** (lagna, Moon, nakshatra, house lords), then **timing** (Vimshottari dasha � transits), then **life-area chapters** (career, marriage, health, wealth, spirituality). Premium Indian PDFs add **16 vargas**, Shadbala, Ashtakavarga, Varshaphal, and remedies ([AstroBasic Kundali](https://astrobasic.com/product/horoscope-kundali-report), [AstroSage Brihat TOC](https://www.astrosage.com/pdf/brihat-horoscope.pdf)).
- **Human synthesis is the differentiator:** Schools and senior practitioners stress that software prints calculations; the astrologer **selects, reconciles, and narrates** � consulting classics, checking divisional charts, and calibrating language to the client ([Hinduism Today / Levacy](https://www.hinduismtoday.com/magazine/october-november-december-2015/2015-10-educational-insight-jyotisha-hindu-astrology/), [Jagannath Hora divisional framework](https://jagannathhora.com/divisional-charts-interpretation-framework/)). Even �automated� reports are often **reviewed or manually written** ([Dr. Palash Thakur](https://palashthhakur.com/comprehensive-astrology-report), [AstroSight Janampatri](https://astrosight.ai/lp/janampatri)).
- **Timing is layered, rarely a single date:** Parashari practice uses **Mahadasha ? Antardasha ? Pratyantar** for multi-year to month-scale windows; **transits (Gochar)** refine inside active dasha, with �double transit� (Jupiter + Saturn) for major life events ([DKSCORE transit framework](https://www.dkscore.com/jyotishmedium/transit-techniques-in-vedic-astrology-three-dimensional-framework-explained-2342), [RoxyAPI Gochar guide](https://roxyapi.com/blogs/planetary-transits-gochar-vedic-astrology-prediction-guide)). KP narrows further via **cuspal Sub-Lords** toward yes/no and shorter windows ([Sai Datta KP blog](https://saidattaastropune.com/blog/kp-astrology/)).
- **Ethics shape content:** Reputable astrologers avoid **death dates, disease diagnosis, and guaranteed outcomes**; they frame health as **timing and vulnerability**, offer **remedies without fear-selling**, and state **~80% accuracy at best** with wrong birth time as the main failure mode ([Hinduism Today](https://www.hinduismtoday.com/magazine/october-november-december-2015/2015-10-educational-insight-jyotisha-hindu-astrology/), [Jagannath Hora ethics code](https://jagannathhora.com/ethical-astrologers-code-guidelines/), [OPA Code of Ethics](https://opaastrology.org/about-opa/code-of-ethics/)).

## 3. Vedic vs Western � Report Structure Differences

| Dimension | Vedic / Jyotish (typical) | Western (typical) |
|-----------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| **Zodiac** | Sidereal + ayanamsa (Lahiri common) | Tropical |
| **Opening triad** | Lagna, Moon (Rashi), Sun � Moon often primary for mind/dasha | Sun, Moon, Rising (Ascendant) � Sun sign culturally dominant |
| **House system** | Whole-sign or Bhava Chalit; KP uses Placidus cusps | Placidus, Koch, Whole Sign (varies) |
| **Core timing engine** | **Vimshottari dasha** (MD/AD/PD/SD); Varshaphal | **Transits + progressions**; solar/lunar returns |
| **Divisional depth** | **D9 mandatory**; D7/D10/D12 etc. by topic | No vargas; optional harmonics/draconic |

## 4. Timing Presentation � How Dasha and Transits Are Phrased

### 4.1 Layered hierarchy (Parashari consensus)

Practitioners describe timing as **nested periods**, not pinpoint timestamps:

1. **Mahadasha (MD)** � �life chapter� (6�20 years per planet): sets dominant themes ([Astronidan dasha guide](https://astronidan.com/blog/dasha-calculator-by-date-of-birth-complete-vimshottari-timeline/), [Hinduism Today � Hathaway on dashas](https://www.hinduismtoday.com/magazine/october-november-december-2015/2015-10-educational-insight-jyotisha-hindu-astrology/)).
2. **Antardasha (AD)** � sub-chapter (months to a few years): blends MD lord with AD lord ([Pragyananda / ParasharaGyanJyoti on dasha-filtered transits](https://pragyananda.com/transit-analysis)).
3. **Pratyantar Dasha (PD)** � weeks to months for event clustering ([Astronidan](https://astronidan.com/blog/dasha-calculator-by-date-of-birth-complete-vimshottari-timeline/)).
4. **Transits (Gochar)** � trigger inside permitted dasha; effective ~**1-year window** for short-term; beyond that, dasha dominates ([DKSCORE](https://www.dkscore.com/jyotishmedium/transit-techniques-in-vedic-astrology-three-dimensional-framework-explained-2342)).

**Metaphor used in teaching:** Dasha = �fixed menu�; transit = �buffet you choose from� ([DKSCORE](https://www.dkscore.com/jyotishmedium/transit-techniques-in-vedic-astrology-three-dimensional-framework-explained-2342)).

### 4.2 Phrasing norms (client-readable)

| Technique | How astrologers phrase it | Example client language |
|-----------|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| MD/AD band | Named periods with start/end dates | �From **June 2024 to late 2026**, you are in **Moon Mahadasha, Rahu Antardasha** � emotional visibility and unconventional paths.� |
| Topic window | Dasha lord of relevant house + supporting MD | �**Marriage is more supported** when Venus or 7th lord periods run, especially if Jupiter transits activate the 7th.� |
| Transit overlay | Planet over natal house/sign from Moon or lagna | �**Saturn transiting your Moon sign** (Sade Sati phase) � pressure on mind and home; not automatic �bad luck,� but a period for patience.� |
| Double transit | Jupiter + Saturn both aspecting 7th/10th etc. | �When **both Jupiter and Saturn** influence your 7th house, marriage or partnership decisions carry extra weight.� ([RoxyAPI](https://roxyapi.com/blogs/planetary-transits-gochar-vedic-astrology-prediction-guide)) |
| KP precision | Sub-Lord on house cusp | �7th cusp Sub-Lord **connected to 2�7�11** ? marriage promised; timing narrowed to **specific AD months** when significators activate.� ([Sai Datta](https://saidattaastropune.com/blog/kp-astrology/)) |
| Uncertainty | Birth time / mixed indications | �These dates assume **�15 minutes** birth accuracy; if lagna shifts, marriage windows move.� |

**Bands vs exact dates:** Commercial PDFs often **print exact dasha start/end dates** from software, but interpretive text uses **months/years bands** (�late 2026�2028,� �first half of Venus AD�). KP-oriented readers may offer **shorter windows** but still rarely promise day-hour precision in natal reports ([Astrology Expert � Vedic vs KP](https://astrology-expert.com/blog/vedic-astrology-vs-kp-astrology)). Live readers explicitly avoid �Tuesday 4pm� unless doing **muhurta/electional** work ([ASTROLOGER_CLIENT_WORKFLOW.md](./ASTROLOGER_CLIENT_WORKFLOW.md) �10).

### 4.3 Divisional timing (advanced)

Marriage timing often cross-checks **D9 Vimshottari**; career uses **D10 dasha** ([Jagannath Hora five-layer method](https://jagannathhora.com/divisional-charts-interpretation-framework/)). ## 6. What They Include / Avoid

### 6.1 Usually included

- Birth time accuracy disclaimer and rectification hints ([Hinduism Today](https://www.hinduismtoday.com/magazine/october-november-december-2015/2015-10-educational-insight-jyotisha-hindu-astrology/))
- Both strengths and challenging indications, framed with compensating factors ([Jagannath Hora ethics � delivering difficult information](https://jagannathhora.com/ethical-astrologers-code-guidelines/))
- **Remedies** when tradition supports: mantra, fasting day, gem with wearing rules, rudraksha ([AstroSight Janampatri](https://astrosight.ai/lp/janampatri), [Life Report Guide � remedies section](https://lifereport.vedicastroanalyst.com/life-report-guide/))
- **Probability language:** �supported,� �likely,� �mixed indications,� �challenging sub-period�
- Limits of astrology vs medical/legal/financial advice ([OPA ethics](https://opaastrology.org/about-opa/code-of-ethics/))

### 6.2 Often excluded or handled carefully

| Topic | Professional norm | Source |
|-------|-------------------|--------|
| **Death prediction** | Declined or only broad longevity discussion; no specific dates | [Jagannath Hora � ethics of prediction](https://jagannathhora.com/ethics-of-prediction-kp-astrology/), [BhagyaVastu health ethics](https://bhagyavastu.com/astrology-services/health-astrology-guidance/) |
| **Medical diagnosis** | Constitutional tendencies, timing for care � **refer to doctors** | [Jagannath Hora KP ethics](https://jagannathhora.com/ethics-of-prediction-kp-astrology/), [AstralLens ethics guide](https://astrallens.art/ethics-morality-astrological-consultations/) |
| **Guaranteed outcomes** | Forbidden; �higher or lower probability� only | [OPA � fatalistic statements](https://opaastrology.org/about-opa/code-of-ethics/) |
| **Fear-based remedy selling** | Unethical; transparent fees | [Jagannath Hora ethics code](https://jagannathhora.com/ethical-astrologers-code-guidelines/), [Hinduism Today � remedial abuse warning](https://www.hinduismtoday.com/magazine/october-november-december-2015/2015-10-educational-insight-jyotisha-hindu-astrology/) |
| **Timeline predictions (some lineages)** | Omitted entirely in natal-only reports | [Saket Bhatia � no dasha/future forecast in Adult Report](https://saketbhatia.com/adult-astrology-report/) |
| **Third-party charts** | Requires consent | [AstralLens ethics](https://astrallens.art/ethics-morality-astrological-consultations/) |

**Notable split:** Dr. Saket Bhatia�s written Adult Report **deliberately excludes Dasha analysis, dosha sections, and remedy/pooja push** � focusing on personality and karmic lessons ([Saket Bhatia](https://saketbhatia.com/adult-astrology-report/)).
