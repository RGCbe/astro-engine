## Part A � What a serious astrologer does (traditional Vedic consultation)

This is the **full study path** a skilled Jyotishi follows before speaking to a client who has already shared birth data (date, time, place). It is not one technique in isolation � it is layered reading, then **synthesis in plain story form**.

### 1. Birth data verification and rectification (if needed)

- Confirms **date, time, timezone, latitude/longitude**, and whether the time is birth-certificate exact, parental memory, or approximate.
- Notes **Rodden rating** or equivalent confidence (A = recorded, DD = dirty data).
- If lagna or life events do not match known milestones (marriage year, first job, death of parent), tests **�15�30 minute** windows and compares event hits across candidates.
- **Never** builds dasha timelines on unverified time.

### 2. Chart construction (rasi and vargas � which and why)

| Chart | Purpose in consultation |
|-------|-------------------------|
| **D1 (Rasi)** | Body, lagna, all house lords, yogas, doshas � foundation |
| **D9 (Navamsa)** | Marriage dharma, spouse nature, hidden strength of planets; **Vimshottari on D9** often used for marriage timing (BPHS) |
| **D10 (Dashamsa)** | Career, public role, authority; **D10 dasha** for profession peaks |
| **D7 (Saptamsa)** | Children, progeny quality |
| **D12 (Dwadashamsa)** | Parents (especially father line from D12 lagna) |
| **D4 (Chaturthamsa)** | Property, fixed assets, emotional roots |
| **D60 (Shashtiamsa)** | Fine karmic grain (advanced readers) |
| **Chandra / Surya lagna** | Moon-centric and Sun-centric alternate readings |
| **Upapada, Arudha** | Marriage image, social perception (Jaimini) |

Whole-sign houses from **sidereal lagna** (Lahiri ayanamsa is standard in South India) are typical for Parashari work.

### 3. Natal reading order (before timing)

Typical spoken sequence:

1. **Lagna and lagna lord** � life direction, constitution  
2. **Moon** � mind, mother, emotional habits; **janma nakshatra** and starting dasha  
3. **Sun** � father, vitality, authority (contextual)  
4. **House lords table** for *this* ascendant (e.g. Sagittarius: 5L Mars, 7L Mercury, 9L Sun)  
5. **Karakas** (Venus spouse, Jupiter children, Sun father, Moon mother, etc.)  
6. **Yogas and doshas** � Raja, Dhana, Neecha Bhanga, Mangal/Kal Sarpa, Pitra/Matra  
7. **Strength** � Shadbala, dignity, combustion, retrograde; Ashtakavarga bindus on sensitive houses  

Only after this foundation does the astrologer interpret **when** things activate.

### 4. Dasha systems (timing backbone)

| System | When used |
|--------|-----------|
| **Vimshottari** | **Primary** � 120-year cycle from Moon nakshatra; MD ? AD ? PD ? SD for life chapters |
| **Chara (Jaimini)** | Sign-based periods; cross-check when Vimshottari is ambiguous; strong for marriage/career *sign* activation |
| **Yogini** | 8-period alternative; some Tamil lineages use for quick temperament of a year |
| **Ashtottari** | 108-year cycle; used when birth is in specific nakshatra/day combinations (not universal) |
| **D9 / D10 Vimshottari** | Marriage and career windows when D1 alone is weak or contradictory |

The astrologer states **which dasha is running now** and which houses that lord rules **for this lagna**.

### 5. Transit layer (Gochara)

- Daily/monthly: planets over natal houses, especially from **Moon** and **lagna**.
- **Sade Sati** � Saturn over Moon sign (7� years); explained as pressure on mind/home, not automatic �bad luck.�
- **Double transit (marriage):** classical rule � **Jupiter and Venus** simultaneously influencing 7th house, 7th lord, or Venus/Moon; tightens marriage windows when dasha already permits.
- Saturn/Mars/Rahu transits to **8th, 12th, 6th** lords � health, loss, conflict themes.

Transits **trigger**; dasha **permits or denies**.

### 6. Divisional deep dives (topic-specific)

| Question | Divisional focus |
|----------|------------------|
| Marriage quality & timing | D9, Upapada, 7th in D1; Venus, 7L |
| Career peak / job change | D10, 10L, Sun in D10 |
| Children | D7, 5th in D1, Jupiter |
| Parents / inheritance | D12, 4th/9th, Sun/Moon |
| Property | D4, 4L |
| Health / accidents | D6, D8, maraka lords |

### 7. Ashtakavarga and Shadbala (when used)

- **Shadbala:** �How strong is this planet�s promise?� � weak 7L dasha ? no marriage, but effort/remedies and delay more likely.
- **Ashtakavarga (BAV/SAV):** bindus in houses � high bindus in 10th support career transits; low in 7th cautions marriage timing.
- Used to **modulate confidence**, not to replace dasha.

### 8. Prashna and muhurta (if relevant)

- **Prashna (horary):** Client asks a urgent question without birth time � separate chart for *moment of question*.
- **Muhurta:** Electional � wedding date, business launch, travel; Panchanga (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana) + Tarabala/Chandrabala.
- Not mixed into natal timing unless the session is explicitly horary/electional.

### 9. Multi-source synthesis (Parashari + Jaimini + KP + Nadi + Bhrigu)

A senior astrologer does **not** stop at one book. Typical reconciliation:

| Tradition | What it contributes |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Parashari (BPHS, Phaladeepika, Saravali)** | Houses, lords, Vimshottari, vargas, yogas |
| **Jaimini** | Chara dasha, karakas (AK/AmK/DK), Argala, Upapada |
| **KP** | Sub-lords, star-lord of cusps, precise event denial when SL contradicts |
| **Nadi (Bhrigu, Brighu Nadi)** | Sign-based karmic bundles, repetitive life themes |
| **Bhrigu Patrika style** | Planet-in-house life chapters for narrative |
| **Tajik / Varshaphala** | Solar return year theme |

**When sources conflict:**

1. Prefer **stronger planet** (Shadbala, varga dignity) over weak duplicate rules.  
2. Require **dasha + transit confluence** for dated predictions.  
3. Treat **corpus contradictions** as �mixed indications� � not pick the flattering line.  
4. KP **denial** (afflicted sub-lord on cusp) can veto a Parashari �looks good� chart.  
5. Jaimini **chara period** agreement with Vimshottari raises confidence.  
6. Cite **2�3 agreeing classics**, mention disagreement honestly.

### 10. How they explain to the client (story, bands, remedies, limits)

**Structure of a good reading:**

1. **Who you are** (lagna, Moon, one yoga) � 2�3 minutes, no jargon avalanche  
2. **Current chapter** � running MD/AD in plain words (�Moon period with Rahu sub-period: emotional visibility + unconventional paths�)  
3. **Topic sections** � marriage, career, family, health � each with **tendency + timing band** (months/years, not �Tuesday 4pm�)  
4. **Both lenses** where fair � e.g. Ketu in 4th: spiritual pull *and* domestic unsettlement  
5. **Remedies** � gemstone/mantra/dana only when tradition and chart support; no fear-selling  
6. **Explicit limits** � no guaranteed lottery wins, death dates, or medical diagnosis; birth time uncertainty stated  

**What they avoid promising:** Exact day of marriage, fixed lifespan, �100% will happen,� or quoting a Sanskrit line that applies to **another lagna**.

### 11. Family events and derived houses (spoken language)

Events in **relatives� lives** appear in the **native�s chart** via houses counted from lagna. The native�s **dasha** often activates when the family event manifests.

| Spoken topic | House logic | Example phrasing |
|--------------|-------------|------------------|
| Father�s health / loss | 9th, 8th from 9th, Sun, 9L | �Your ninth house and Sun show your father�s chapter; when your 9th lord dasha runs with difficult transit, we watch his health.� |
| Mother�s chapter | 4th, Moon, 4L | �Fourth house and Moon are mother; Saturn over Moon can stress her peace, not only yours.� |
| Sibling marriage | 3rd, 8th from 3rd (10th) | �Third is siblings; their marriage often lights your 10th-from-3rd house during your 3rd lord period.� |
| **Maternal uncle** | **6th** (3rd from 4th) | �Sixth house is mother�s younger circle � maternal uncle themes.� |
| **Paternal uncle** | **11th** (3rd from 9th) | �Eleventh from lagna � father�s sibling line.� |
| Father-in-law | 3rd (9th from 7th) | �Third house also carries spouse�s father.� |
| Mother-in-law | 10th (4th from 7th) | �Tenth house � spouse�s mother.� |
| Child born to native | 5th, Jupiter, 5L | �Fifth house children; Jupiter�s period is the classic window.� |
| Spouse�s career event | 7th, 10th from 7th (4th) | �Your fourth house is partner�s career house.� |

**Linked charts (spouse/child JSON)** give the cleanest �vice versa� story; single-chart reading uses **derived houses** only.

## Part C � Ideal client session script (outline for Manoj)

**Fixture:** `charts/manoj_chart.json` � 1996-05-25 20:25 Coimbatore, **Sagittarius lagna**, **Magha** Moon, Ketu MD at birth ? **Moon MD / Rahu AD** in 2026.  
This is **structure only** � not a prediction. An astrologer who completed a full multi-source study would cover something like:

1. **Open:** Confirm birth time source (certificate vs memory); state that wrong time changes lagna and all dates.  
2. **Chart snapshot:** �Sagittarius rising � Jupiter in the first gives strong identity and dharma bent; Moon in the ninth links mind to belief, travel, father-guru themes.�  
3. **Running period:** �You are in Moon Mahadasha with Rahu Antardasha � visibility, emotional churn, unconventional paths; sub-period Mars now adds heat and decisiveness for a few weeks.�  
4. **Strength context:** �Jupiter as lagna lord in own sign is your anchor; Saturn�Ketu in the fourth needs patience on home/mother peace, not fear.�  
5. **Marriage chapter:** D1 seventh house (Gemini, Venus), **D9** navamsa check, Venus/7L dasha windows; mention if classics **agree or split**; timing as **bands** only.  
6. **Career chapter:** Tenth house Rahu, **D10** dashamsa lord periods, Jupiter transit notes � promotion vs change vs study.  
7. **Wealth / property:** Second and eleventh lords, Dhana yogas present, realistic peaks vs caution periods.  
8. **Family � father (Sun, ninth lord in sixth):** Neutral vs stress sub-periods; derived ninth-house language, not medical diagnosis.  
9. **Family � mother (fourth, Saturn�Ketu):** Home comfort theme; Sade Sati relevance if Saturn transiting Moon sign.  
10. **Siblings / extended family:** Third and eleventh houses; maternal uncle (sixth from fourth) only if client asked.  
11. **Health sensitivity:** Sixth house Sun, Mars periods � preventive care language, not disease names as fate.  
12. **Spirituality / remedies:** Fifth-ninth axis, Ketu heritage; remedies tied to afflicted lords, optional.  
13. **Next 12�18 months:** Top 3�5 **mechanical bands** from `event_predictor_v2` (career, relationship, wealth, health, father) with severity = strength of signal.  
14. **Classical footnotes:** 2�3 corpus lines that match **this** lagna/placement, with book names (BPHS, Phaladeepika, KP, Bhrigu).  
15. **Close:** Recap �chapter + topic bands,� disclaim uncertainty, invite feedback when events pass to refine time.
